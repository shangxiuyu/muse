生成方式：内置 image_gen。

设计方向：以人物侧脸和展开的纸页共用一个轮廓；用负空间表现感知者与作品的关系。沿用品牌墨绿与纸白。

## 最终生成提示词

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Design one imaginative, beautifully minimal brand icon for MUSE, an aesthetic intelligence and creative studio. Concept: "The Muse hidden in the page" — a human profile discovered inside a sculptural paper-like form. This must be a sophisticated memorable pictorial symbol, not a letter.

Main symbol: a single large ivory organic silhouette, almost circular overall. It simultaneously evokes a classical muse's head in right-facing profile and three broad unfurling pages. The left and top form a generous rounded head of flowing hair, divided by only TWO broad dark-green curved cuts into THREE sweeping petal/page forms. The right edge of this ivory mass has a subtly sculpted but immediately readable forehead, small dignified nose, gentle lips and chin in profile, cut from the surrounding green negative space. A graceful short neck curves back toward the base, making one compact unified emblem. The face is a simple silhouette with NO drawn eye, no eyebrow, no internal facial details. The flowing hair/page structure should feel inventive, cultivated, relaxed, not heraldic. Integrate the three page forms into the head itself, not objects sitting on the head. No flower crown.

Design priorities: silhouette works at 32px; no narrow details, thin strands or ornamental linework; clever figure-ground reading; asymmetrical flowing profile balanced inside a compact nearly round footprint. Generous curves with crisp intentional terminations. An artistic publishing imprint or museum identity, distilled into a friendly modern app icon. Distinctive without complexity.

Colors: solid warm ivory #FAF9F6 emblem on a completely opaque uniform ivy-green #315847 square. Exactly two flat colors. Green fills all four corners. No transparency.
Composition: one large symbol optically centered, about 62% canvas width and 65% canvas height, square 1:1. No surrounding badge or border, no rounded square tile, no presentation layout.
Medium: pure 2D flat graphic design, immaculate vector-like edges. No gradients, no bevels, no shadows, no texture, no 3D, no mockups, no lettering, no watermark. One final usable icon image.
```
