# Muse 图标 · 灵感小脑袋

用户确认方向：手绘风格，简洁、大胆有趣。

生成方式：内置 image_gen。

设计方向：粗黑手绘线条、暖黄色背景和米白小脑袋；用一笔卷毛及略带俏皮的表情表达创作伙伴的性格。配色为此次新方向的设计提案。

## 首轮生成提示词

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Create one finished square app icon for MUSE, a creative companion for people with taste. The user explicitly wants HAND-DRAWN, SIMPLE, BOLD, FUN. Make it feel like an irresistibly memorable doodle by a confident independent illustrator.

Creative concept: "a little head full of ideas". A single oversized, charming, slightly wonky face drawn with a thick almost-black ink marker on a solid butter-yellow square. The head is an off-white irregular rounded pear/blob, a little wider at the bottom, angled very slightly. A SINGLE playful large curling hair stroke rises from the top like an idea taking shape — a loose open curl, NOT a lightbulb or a star. Keep it one decisive expressive curl, attached to the head. The face has just TWO small unevenly placed solid black oval eyes, glancing a little sideways, and one tiny off-center crooked smile. A curious, clever, mischievous little creative friend. Optional only one subtle tiny coral circular cheek mark if it truly improves the character; do not add anything else.

Draw boldly and economically: one chunky slightly wobbly head outline with a natural marker edge, one curl, two eye dots and one short smile stroke. Heavy blunt line endings. Visible handmade irregularity but clean intentional silhouette, no scratchy sketch construction lines. Strong distinctive proportions: very large quiet cheek area, tiny widely spaced eyes, curl with personality. No body, no arms, no accessories, no nose, no eyelashes, no lettering, no separate decorative symbols. It should be appealing and absurd in a restrained way, NOT a generic emoji face, not a conventional corporate mascot. Seek warmth, wit and individuality.

Color palette: saturated warm butter yellow #F4C84A fully opaque flat background; near-black ink #242520; warm off-white #FFF8E8 face fill; optional tiny coral #E76A4A cheek. Background extends to all four square corners; no transparent area. Bold high contrast.
Composition: one head and its attached curl optically centered, entire subject takes roughly 68 percent canvas width and 72 percent height with comfortable margin. Square 1:1. No inset app tile or enclosing border. The head remains clearly legible at 32px.
Medium: contemporary hand-drawn editorial doodle icon, thick black marker and flat color fills, very simple, bold, fun. No gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no glossy rendering, no bevel, no paper grain or distress overlay, no detailed texture, no mockup, no text or watermark. Produce one finished icon, not a sheet of options.
```
